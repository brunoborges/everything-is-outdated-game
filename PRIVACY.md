# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 24, 2026_

This policy covers [everythingisoutdated.dev](https://everythingisoutdated.dev) ("the site"), a free browser game by [@brunoborges](https://github.com/brunoborges).

## What the game itself stores

- **Game session.** When you play, the site creates a random, anonymous session ID. It is kept in your browser's `localStorage` and on the server with your in-game progress (commands run, packages "upgraded", elapsed time). Sessions expire after 7 days of inactivity. "Reboot machine" starts a new one.
- **Theme preference.** Your light/dark choice is saved in `localStorage`.
- **No accounts.** The game does not ask for your name, email, or any other personal information.
- **Hosting logs.** The site runs on Cloudflare, which may process technical data such as IP addresses to deliver and protect the service. See the [Cloudflare Privacy Policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

Commands you type are sent to the server only to run the simulated terminal. Please don't type real secrets into it.

## Analytics

The site uses **Google Tag Manager** and **Google Analytics** to understand aggregate usage, such as visits, pages, and general location. These services may set cookies and collect your IP address, browser, and device information. See [How Google uses information from sites that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## Advertising

The site shows ads served by **Google AdSense**.

- Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites.
- Google's advertising cookies let Google and its partners serve ads based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
- You can opt out of personalized advertising in [Google Ads Settings](https://www.google.com/settings/ads). You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' cookies at [www.aboutads.info/choices](https://www.aboutads.info/choices/).

Visitors in the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalized ads or cookies are used, as required by law.

## Your choices

- Clear your browser's site data to remove the game session and preferences.
- Block or delete cookies in your browser settings.
- Use the opt-out links above for advertising.

## Children

The site is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.

## Changes and contact

This policy may be updated from time to time; changes will be committed to this repository. For questions, open an issue in [this repository](https://github.com/brunoborges/everything-is-outdated-game/issues).
